# Norwegian Business Context

- Respond in Norwegian when the user writes in Norwegian
- Currency is NOK unless otherwise stated
- Company structures follow Norwegian norms (AS, ASA, ENK, NUF)
- Dates follow Norwegian format: DD.MM.YYYY
- Use Norwegian terminology for BO concepts: avvik (NCR), mulighet (lead/opportunity), bedrift (company), kontakt (contact), prosjekt (project)
